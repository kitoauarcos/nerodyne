How it works

A confidential model. Actionable research.

Vortex is a systematic strategy whose methodology is proprietary to Nerodyne and is not published. Subscribers receive what is needed to act on it: the positions the strategy holds and when they change, the corresponding put option hedge, and the full backtest. You provide the brokerage account and execute the trades.

1. The model evaluates

The strategy screens the US market on a fixed schedule using its proprietary ruleset, combining quality, growth, momentum and risk criteria. The methodology is not disclosed.

2. The change is detected

The model is re-run and compared against the current portfolio. Most of the time there is no change. When there is, whether a new position, a weight change, or a hedge adjustment, it is identified.

3. You receive the update

You receive the new target portfolio, with tickers, weights, and any hedge components. You execute in your own brokerage account, retaining full control of execution and custody.

The hedge layer

Optional downside protection

Vortex is offered with or without a hedge. The protected versions add a permanent put option overlay: a standing index (QQQ) put ladder, with the concentrated Vortex Shield also holding protective puts on its individual positions, and the Diversified Shield using the index ladder alone across a broader book of quality companies, the biggest stock in the world and a dividend-leaders ETF sleeve. The overlay reduces losses in a market decline in exchange for some return in calm periods.

In the two weakest markets of the sample the hedge turned the crash into a gain: 2020 +64% and 2022 +16% for Vortex Shield, versus −18% for the S&P 500 in 2022. The cost shows up in calm years, where the option premium drags on returns. You choose the trade-off per model.

Three ways to run it

Vortex, pure, shielded or diversified

Pure

Vortex

The strategy at full strength: at each rebalance it pools the holdings of six internal models, keeps only companies whose revenue is growing or stable, and concentrates into the five most mutually distinct businesses. Highest return in the backtest.

Hedged

Vortex Shield

The same book wrapped in a permanent put-option hedge: an always-on QQQ ladder plus protective puts on the holdings. The safety-first version: less return in calm years, but the convex hedge can turn a crash year strongly positive.

Diversified

Diversified Shield

A broader book of 15 or more quality companies: durable margins and a positive track record over 3, 5 and 10 years. It always holds the biggest stock in the world by market value, plus a developed-markets dividend-leaders ETF sleeve, and is hedged with the index put ladder.
